Making

Capybara Parade Beanie Pattern

Original Pattern · Knitting · Colourwork · Fair Isle style

is a computational research project investigating how successful robotic rearrangements alter the accessibility of shared environments for subsequent users.

It examines the consequences of task-complete spatial configurations, the availability of less disruptive alternatives, and the differences between aggregate accessibility improvements and individual route requirements.
