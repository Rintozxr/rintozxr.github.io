How My Questions Evolved

One question led to another.

It started with observing people in real places. Then the questions got a little more complicated.

01

What can we learn about human experience from environmental measurements?

Blue-Space Soundscape · 2024

Field observations revealed that restorative environments do not necessarily encourage more social interaction.

Explore project →
02

And what happens when we replace direct measurements with computational proxies?

StreetSignals · 2025

Street-view imagery and computational methods opened possibilities for examining urban environments at a larger scale, while raising questions about what visible features can represent.

Explore project →
03

But what do we miss when we describe environments through their visible components rather than their spatial relationships?

Beyond Semantics · 2025

Visually similar environments may differ in how their elements are organized and how people use them. This proposal explores spatial structure as a complement to component-based environmental representations.

Explore project →
04

But can any environmental assessment be meaningful without considering who uses the space?

HumanRoute · 2026

The same walking environment may mean very different things to people with different mobility needs. Can multimodal models account for those differences?

Explore project →
05

And even when individual needs are known, what makes one spatial outcome better than another?

AFTERMATH · 2026

A robot can successfully complete its task while leaving behind a space that no longer works for everyone.
